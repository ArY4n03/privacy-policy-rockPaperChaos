
# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 30, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **[Rock Paper Chaos]** ("the Game", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Game.

## 1. Information We Collect

We do **not directly collect, store, or request any personal information from users** through the Game.

The Game does not require users to create an account or provide information such as their name, email address, phone number, or physical address.

## 2. Advertising

The Game uses **Unity Ads**, provided by Unity Technologies, to display advertisements.

Although we do not directly collect or store personal information, Unity Ads may automatically process certain information from your device for advertising and related purposes. This may include device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location, and information related to advertisements and their interactions.

The information processed by Unity Ads is subject to Unity's own privacy policies.

For more information, please see Unity's Privacy Policy:

https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

## 3. Third-Party Services

The Game uses Unity Ads as a third-party advertising service.

We do not control the information that Unity may process through its advertising services. Users should refer to Unity's privacy policy for information about how Unity handles data.

## 4. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the Game.

If you believe that personal information has been provided to us through the Game, please contact us using the contact information below.

## 5. Data Security

Since we do not directly collect or store personal information through the Game, we do not maintain a database of user personal information.

Information that may be processed by third-party services such as Unity Ads is handled according to those services' respective privacy policies.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page, along with an updated "Last updated" date.
